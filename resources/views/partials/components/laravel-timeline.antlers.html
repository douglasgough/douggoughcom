Why Laravel?

When you’re making a long-term platform decision, the foundation matters. Laravel isn’t an emerging framework—it’s the most widely adopted PHP framework in the world, with a trajectory that gives organizations serious confidence in its future.

  1. 2011

    Laravel Is Born

    Taylor Otwell releases Laravel as a clean, modern alternative to older PHP frameworks. Developers notice immediately.

  2. 2015–2018

    Rapid Adoption

    Laravel becomes the most popular PHP framework on GitHub. Its ecosystem of packages, tools, and community resources grows rapidly.

  3. 2020–2023

    Enterprise Adoption Doubles

    The number of websites running on Laravel nearly doubles. Organizations of every size—from startups to healthcare enterprises—adopt it for mission-critical applications.

  4. 2024

    $57M Investment from Accel Milestone

    One of the world's leading technology venture firms invests in Laravel's future. This is a platform with serious long-term backing.

  5. 2025

    Laravel 12 Released

    A stability-focused release signals framework maturity. Over 1.5 million websites now run on Laravel, with adoption continuing to grow.
