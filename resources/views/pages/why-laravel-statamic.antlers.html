Why Laravel + Statamic

When you're making a long-term platform decision, the foundation matters. Here's why Laravel and Statamic are the right choice for organizations that have outgrown WordPress but don't need the weight of an enterprise CMS.

Why Laravel?

When you're making a long-term platform decision, the foundation matters.

Widely Adopted

Laravel is the most popular PHP framework in the world, powering over 1.5 million websites across healthcare, finance, education, and enterprise applications.

Battle-Tested

Adoption has nearly doubled since 2020. Organizations of every size trust Laravel for complex, mission-critical applications.

Serious Backing

In 2024, Laravel received a $57 million investment from Accel—one of the world's leading technology venture firms. This is a platform with a long future.

Vast Ecosystem

Because Laravel is so widely adopted, there's an enormous library of pre-built packages for almost any functionality you need—without building from scratch.

Never Locked In

If you ever need to bring in additional help, Laravel developers are abundant. You're never locked into a single vendor.

Why Statamic?

Statamic isn't the biggest CMS on the market. It's the right CMS for a specific kind of project—and that's exactly the point.

Built for Editors

Statamic's control panel was designed with content editors in mind. Clean, intuitive, and adaptable to your content structure—not the other way around.

Built on Laravel

Statamic isn't a standalone CMS with its own proprietary framework underneath. It's a content management layer built directly on Laravel—which means it inherits Laravel's security, stability, and ecosystem from the ground up.

Flexible Content Modelling

With over 40 field types and a blueprint-based content architecture, Statamic can model almost any content structure your organization needs—without the complexity of an enterprise CMS.

Flat-File by Default

No database required. Content is stored in files, which means simpler hosting, easier backups, and Git-based version control of your content as well as your code.

Multilingual

Full multilingual support built in—not bolted on.

Actively Developed

Statamic is under active development—now at version 6—with regular releases, strong documentation, and a growing community of developers on GitHub and Discord.

A Deliberate Choice, Not a Compromise

Statamic is a deliberate choice, not a compromise. For organizations that need structured content, a great editor experience, and room to grow on a solid Laravel foundation—it's the right tool for the job.
