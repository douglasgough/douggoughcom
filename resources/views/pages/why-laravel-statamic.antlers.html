Why Laravel?

When you’re making a long-term platform decision, the foundation matters. Laravel isn’t an emerging framework—it’s the most widely adopted PHP framework in the world, with a trajectory that gives organizations serious confidence in its future.

  1. 2011

    Laravel Is Born

    Taylor Otwell releases Laravel as a clean, modern alternative to older PHP frameworks. Developers notice immediately.

  2. 2015–2018

    Rapid Adoption

    Laravel becomes the most popular PHP framework on GitHub. Its ecosystem of packages, tools, and community resources grows rapidly.

  3. 2020–2023

    Enterprise Adoption Doubles

    The number of websites running on Laravel nearly doubles. Organizations of every size—from startups to healthcare enterprises—adopt it for mission-critical applications.

  4. 2024

    $57M Investment from Accel Milestone

    One of the world's leading technology venture firms invests in Laravel's future. This is a platform with serious long-term backing.

  5. 2025

    Laravel 12 Released

    A stability-focused release signals framework maturity. Over 1.5 million websites now run on Laravel, with adoption continuing to grow.

Why Statamic?

Statamic is a deliberate specialization. It was built for organizations that need structured content, a great editor experience, and room to grow—on a foundation that can support whatever comes next.

Built for Editors

The control panel was designed with content editors in mind—clean, intuitive, and adaptable to your content structure. With 40+ field types, Statamic models the way your organization actually works, not the other way around.

Built-In Where Others Use Plugins

Statamic includes functionality that other CMS platforms require plugins for—multilingual support, content versioning, flexible content modeling, user permissions. Plugins have to be maintained, updated, and kept compatible. Statamic ships with it.

Version 6. A Dedicated Team.

Statamic is on version 6, under active development by a dedicated team of employees—not assembled from community contributions. Regular releases, strong documentation, and a roadmap with real accountability behind it.

Your Content Is Always Yours

Content is stored in files, version-controlled alongside your code. That means no database breach risk, automatic revision history, and content that’s never locked inside a proprietary system. If you ever need to move, you can.

Built on Laravel

Statamic isn’t a standalone CMS with its own framework underneath. It’s a content management layer built directly on Laravel—which means any Laravel developer can work with it, and your site can grow into custom applications without ever changing platforms.

Built for Where You Are — and Where You're Going

Organizations that build on Laravel and Statamic stop fighting their website and start using it. Here’s what that looks like in practice.

  • Editors who dreaded your CMS will actually use this one. Statamic’s control panel is clean, intuitive, and built around your content structure—not the other way around. A site that gets updated works harder for your organization.
  • Simple changes stay simple. Day-to-day updates don’t require a developer or a support ticket. Your team stays in control without outside help for routine work.
  • Your platform grows with your ambitions. Built on Laravel, your site can expand into membership tools, custom applications, and partner integrations—without rebuilding from scratch when you’re ready for the next phase.
  • Your content is always secure and always yours. No database to breach, automatic revision history built in, and content that’s never locked inside a proprietary system.
  • One developer who knows your system deeply. Not a ticket queue. Not a new contact every engagement. Consistent support from someone invested in your site for the long term.
