Why Laravel + Statamic

When you're making a long-term platform decision, the foundation matters. Here's why we build on Laravel and Statamic.

Why Laravel?

  1. 2011

    Laravel Is Born

    Taylor Otwell releases Laravel as a clean, modern alternative to older PHP frameworks. Developers notice immediately.

  2. 2015–2018

    Rapid Adoption

    Laravel becomes the most popular PHP framework on GitHub. Its ecosystem of packages, tools, and community resources grows rapidly.

  3. 2020–2023

    Enterprise Adoption Doubles

    The number of websites running on Laravel nearly doubles. Organizations of every size—from startups to healthcare enterprises—adopt it for mission-critical applications.

  4. 2024

    $57M Investment from Accel Milestone

    One of the world's leading technology venture firms invests in Laravel's future. This is a platform with serious long-term backing.

  5. 2025

    Laravel 12 Released

    A stability-focused release signals framework maturity. Over 1.5 million websites now run on Laravel, with adoption continuing to grow.

Why Statamic?

Statamic isn't the biggest CMS on the market. It's the right CMS for a specific kind of project—and that's exactly the point.

Built for Editors

Statamic's control panel was designed with content editors in mind. Clean, intuitive, and adaptable to your content structure—not the other way around.

40+ field types. An editing experience your team will actually use.

Built on Laravel

Statamic isn't a standalone CMS with its own proprietary framework underneath. It's a content management layer built directly on Laravel—which means it inherits Laravel's security, stability, and ecosystem from the ground up.

Not a proprietary stack. Any Laravel developer can work with it.

Flexible Content Modelling

With over 40 field types and a blueprint-based content architecture, Statamic can model almost any content structure your organization needs—without the complexity of an enterprise CMS.

Model complex content relationships without writing custom code.

Flat-File by Default

No database required. Content is stored in files, which means simpler hosting, easier backups, and Git-based version control of your content as well as your code.

No database. Content lives in files—version controlled alongside your code.

Multilingual

Full multilingual support built in—not bolted on.

Built-in, not bolted on. Full multilingual support since version 3.

Actively Developed

Statamic is under active development—now at version 6—with regular releases, strong documentation, and a growing community of developers on GitHub and Discord.

Version 6. Regular releases. A team invested in making it better.

A Deliberate Choice, Not a Compromise

Statamic is a deliberate choice, not a compromise. For organizations that need structured content, a great editor experience, and room to grow on a solid Laravel foundation—it's the right tool for the job.
