What Web Stewardship Includes

Stability & Maintenance

Reliable hosting, updates, backups, and security handled responsibly—so problems don't pile up.

Clear Responsibility

Defined ownership, plain-language documentation, and clear processes—so continuity doesn't depend on one person's memory.

Incremental Improvement

Small, safe changes that solve real problems without unnecessary disruption or rebuild cycles.

Support for Your Team

Practical training and clear handoffs that respect staff and volunteer capacity.

When a New Build Makes Sense

Most problems don't require a rebuild. But when the foundation is no longer safe or maintainable—or the church is growing significantly—a new build may be the most responsible next step. I'll tell you honestly which situation you're in.

How We Work Together

We start with a short conversation to understand your situation. I gather access, stabilize anything urgent, and give you a clear recommended next step in plain language. Work moves forward with documented decisions, small safe changes, and predictable outcomes.

I need one point of contact, clear access to your systems, and a simple written agreement that keeps scope and responsibilities clear.
